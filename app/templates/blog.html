01 / Context

The Problem

Customer support teams in furniture and manufacturing businesses receive repetitive emails regarding damaged products, missing components, order issues, and replacement requests.

Traditional Workflow

  • — Manually reading incoming emails
  • — Searching product documentation
  • — Reviewing customer-submitted images
  • — Writing individual responses
  • — Tracking every support request

Engineering Objective

  • — Automate email understanding
  • — Extract information from images
  • — Retrieve relevant product knowledge
  • — Generate contextual responses
  • — Preserve human decision-making

04 / Pipeline

Email Ingestion

The Gmail integration periodically checks the configured mailbox for new customer messages. Incoming messages are parsed and converted into tickets and conversation records.

Gmail Inbox ↓ Fetch Recent Messages ↓ Extract Sender, Subject & Body ↓ Process Attachments ↓ Create / Update Ticket ↓ Persist in PostgreSQL

Important Engineering Considerations

Email identifiers, message history, duplicate processing, and reply detection must be handled carefully to maintain consistent conversations.

07 / Reliability

AI Guardrails

10 / Execution

Background Processing

Background polling and asynchronous processing allow incoming email operations to run independently from dashboard requests.

11 / Data Integrity

Concurrency & Transaction Safety

Multiple workers or repeated processing attempts can potentially claim the same event. Atomic database operations help prevent duplicate processing and dispatch.

Engineering Principles

  • — Atomic event claiming
  • — Transaction boundaries
  • — Duplicate event prevention
  • — Consistent ticket state transitions
  • — Safe email dispatch handling
12 / Performance

Performance & Optimization

Database Connection Pooling

Reusing PostgreSQL connections through psycopg-pool reduces connection establishment overhead and avoids repeatedly opening new connections.

Vector Indexing

HNSW indexing enables efficient approximate nearest neighbor searches for vector-based retrieval.

Email Loop Prevention

RFC-related headers such as Auto-Submitted and Precedence can help prevent automated replies from being interpreted as new customer requests.

16 / Retrospective

Engineering Decisions & Learnings

Building this system required more than integrating an LLM. It involved designing reliable workflows around external APIs, database consistency, email delivery, and AI-generated content.

Reliability over AI autonomy

Human approval provides a practical control point for customer-facing communication.

Database correctness matters

Concurrency and duplicate processing must be considered in background workflows.

Context improves AI responses

Retrieval-based context makes product-specific answers more grounded.
