
# Orders and Installation

## Product enquiries
Customers can enquire about individual products, bulk furniture orders, and complete office furnishing projects.

Collect relevant information:
- Customer name.
- Product name.
- Required quantity.
- Preferred dimensions.
- Material and colour preferences.
- Delivery location.
- Customization requirements.

## Pricing and quotations
Pricing depends on product specifications, dimensions, materials, quantity, customization, and project requirements.

Never invent prices or provide unapproved quotations.

A colleague reviews quotation requests and provides confirmed pricing.

## Orders
For order-related enquiries, collect:
- Order number.
- Customer name.
- Product name.
- Order date, if available.
- Description of the enquiry.

Do not promise order changes, cancellations, or replacements.

## Delivery
Delivery arrangements depend on product type, order quantity, destination, and project requirements.

Never promise a delivery date without confirmation from a colleague.

## Installation and assembly
Some products may require assembly or professional installation.

Installation requirements depend on:
- Product type.
- Quantity.
- Project location.
- Site conditions.
- Assembly complexity.

Do not assume installation is included with every order.

## Office project installation
Large projects involving office cabins, partitions, workstations, and complete office interiors require coordination.

Collect:
- Project location.
- Site photographs.
- Floor plan, if available.
- Office dimensions.
- Required furniture.
- Expected project schedule.

A colleague reviews the project and coordinates the next steps.

## Damaged or missing products
If a customer reports damaged, missing, or incorrect furniture, collect:
- Order number.
- Product name.
- Description of the issue.
- Photographs, if available.

A colleague reviews the complaint and confirms the appropriate resolution.
