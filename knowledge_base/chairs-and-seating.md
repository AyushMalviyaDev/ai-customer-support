## Seating customization
Seating requirements may include:
- Chair type.
- Seating capacity.
- Colour and finish.
- Upholstery preferences.
- Material preferences.
- Quantity.
- Intended usage.

Do not guarantee specific materials, models, or features unless confirmed in product information.

## Recommendations
When recommending seating, understand:
- Where the furniture will be used.
- How many chairs or seats are needed.
- Whether the requirement is for employees, executives, or visitors.
- Preferred design and colour.
- Available budget.

## Bulk seating orders
For bulk orders, collect the required quantity, delivery location, preferred product category, and any customization requirements.

A colleague confirms pricing, availability, and order details.
