## Office partitions
Partition solutions can help divide office spaces into separate functional areas.

Available options may include:
- Glass partitions.
- Aluminium frame partitions.
- Wooden partitions.
- Modular partitions.
- Office cubicle partitions.
- Privacy partitions.

The suitable solution depends on space, design, privacy, and project requirements.

## Project information
For office interior enquiries, collect:
- Company name.
- Customer name.
- Project location.
- Approximate office area.
- Number of employees.
- Number of cabins required.
- Required furniture.
- Preferred design.
- Budget, if available.
- Floor plan or site photographs, if available.
