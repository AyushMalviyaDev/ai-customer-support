# Escalation Rules

## Always escalate triggers
A ticket must be escalated to a human colleague whenever the customer:
- Reports damaged, broken, missing, or defective furniture.
- Requests a refund, payment dispute, cancellation, or compensation.
- Asks for a phone call, physical site visit, or meeting with a manager.
- Is dissatisfied, angry, or threatening negative reviews or legal action.
- Inquires about the delay or exact live status of an existing order.
- Requests custom-manufactured furniture designs or bulk turnkey office interior quotations.
- Inquires about on-site installation scheduling or warranty claims.

## Disallowed promises
Support agents must NEVER commit to or promise any of the following directly:
- Exact delivery dates or rush delivery promises.
- Financial discounts, price reductions, or custom pricing.
- Free replacement units or immediate refunds.
- Unscheduled on-site technician visits.
A human specialist from our logistics or sales team will review and confirm these arrangements.
