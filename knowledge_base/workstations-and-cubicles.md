
# Workstations and Cubicles

## Workstation solutions
We provide workstation furniture for modern offices and commercial environments.

Common configurations include:
- Single-person workstations.
- Two-person workstations.
- Four-person workstation clusters.
- Six-person workstation clusters.
- Open office workstations.
- Manager workstations.
- Customized employee workstations.

The right configuration depends on office space, employee count, workflow, and budget.

## Office cubicles
We provide cubicle and partition solutions for businesses that need organized employee workspaces.

Options include:
- Modular office cubicles.
- Individual employee cubicles.
- Team workstation cubicles.
- Privacy partitions.
- Glass partitions.
- Desk-mounted partitions.
- Customized cubicle layouts.

## Workstation customization
Workstations may be customized according to:
- Desk dimensions.
- Number of employees.
- Partition height.
- Material and colour.
- Storage and drawers.
- Cable management.
- Privacy requirements.
- Office layout.

Do not guarantee customization options without confirmation from a colleague.

## Information required for enquiries
For workstation and cubicle enquiries, collect:
- Customer or company name.
- Project location.
- Number of employees.
- Number of workstations required.
- Approximate office dimensions.
- Preferred workstation configuration.
- Material and colour preferences.
- Budget, if available.

## Complete workstation projects
For large office projects, customers can share floor plans, site photographs, and layout requirements.

A colleague reviews the requirements and handles the quotation and project discussion.
